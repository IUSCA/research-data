# Peer policies and frameworks

Checked 2026-10-04. These are examples from outside IU. None of them binds
IU researchers. Use them to explain why a Recommended practice is reasonable,
or to show what an IU policy might say.

## University of Michigan, SPG 303.06, Research Data Stewardship Policy

Issued June 27, 2023; effective January 1, 2024. Owned by the Office of the
Vice President for Research.

- **Ownership.** "regardless of location, intent, or funding source, the
  University owns any resultant Research Data."
- **Retention.** "a minimum of seven years after final closeout of a project
  or publication of the data, whichever occurs later."
- **Departure.** Original data "remain at the University." Copies of
  non-public data leave through "a data transfer and use agreement."

## Duke University, Research Policy Manual, Chapter 5: Research Data

Approved December 15, 2022; effective May 1, 2023.

- **Ownership.** Duke owns research records "collected, generated, or
  created during research conducted" within the scope of duties, with
  University resources, or with University funding.
- **Retention.** "a minimum of six years after the final reporting or
  publication of a project."
- **Departure.** Duke keeps ownership. Researchers request access "through
  proper University channels."

## Purdue University, Rossmann

Purdue's research computing center announced Rossmann on January 27, 2025. It
is a central system "fully implementing the NIST 800-171 standard" for NIH
genomic data under NOT-OD-24-157. New DUAs for NIH genomic data are assigned
to it. It shows one way to meet the controlled-access requirements in
`navigating-research-data-policy`.

## NIST Research Data Framework (RDaF) v2.0

NIST SP 1500-18r2, February 2024. A voluntary, consensus framework. Its six
lifecycle stages are Envision, Plan, Generate/Acquire, Process/Analyze,
Share/Use/Reuse, and Preserve/Discard. It can serve as a shared vocabulary
between research, IT, and library staff.

## RDA Global Open Research Commons (GORC) International Model

A Research Data Alliance working group output from November 2023. It is "a
set of attributes for GORC organisations to measure their user engagement and
development internally within the organisation, gauge their maturity and
compare features across commons." It can serve as a maturity rubric for a
research data commons such as the RDC.
