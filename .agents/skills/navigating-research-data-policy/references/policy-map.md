# What binds today, by topic

Checked 2026-10-04. This map lists, for each part of a research data policy,
the binding source if one exists, the IU guidance, and the gap. It follows
the elements a research data policy usually covers. Michigan's SPG 303.06 and
Duke's Research Policy Manual chapter 5 are examples.

| Topic | Binding today | IU guidance | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Definitions | UA-23 defines Research Data; DM-01 defines institutional data | Data Management FAQ on research data | No single definition across policies |
| Ownership | IU Data Management governance (Board of Trustees); UA-23; UA-24; HRPP policy for human subjects | Data Management FAQ on classifying research data | No general statement for research outside human subjects; agreements override |
| Classification | DM-01, DM-01-S; the Data Classification Matrix sets research data to Restricted by default | FAQ steps; DSH tool | Covered |
| Access rights | DM-01-S, in part | Account and access procedures | Who beyond the project may access research data, and disputes within a team |
| Storage and handling | DM-01-S; KB service approvals; IT-12 | IU Guidance for the Management of Research Data (2019); Data Storage Finder | Guidance is dated; Storage Finder and KB disagree on Critical data |
| Retention | 2 CFR 200.334 for federal awards; HRPP policy for human subjects; UA-18 for university records; sponsor terms | 2019 guidance: 5 years | No general minimum for research data |
| Disposal | Agreements and sponsor terms | None specific to research data | No procedure |
| Archiving and preservation | None | 2019 guidance; IU Libraries | No policy |
| Transfer and sharing | DM-02; third-party assessment; ORA signs agreements | RDC Pathways for Sharing Research Data | When an agreement is required for outside collaborators; departing researchers |
| Controlled-access data | NIH GDS security requirements (NOT-OD-24-157), through agreements | IU NIH GDS update page | IU's designated systems are not named publicly |
| AI tools | KB0026817 | KB0027312 | Covered for current tools |
| Data management plans | Sponsor terms (NIH, NSF) | IU NIH DMS guidance; RDC consultations | No requirement for unfunded work |
| Sanctions | IT-02, through DM-01 | — | Nothing specific to research data |

How to use this map:

- Where the "Binding today" column names a source, the skills mark the
  matching statements as Required.
- Where it says "None" or names only guidance, the skills mark practice as
  **Recommended**, and name the gap as an open item.
- When IU adopts a research data policy, update this table first, then the
  skills.
