# Getting help with research data

Verified 2026-10-04 against the IU pages named in Sources.

Send each question to the office that decides it. The RDC is the general
front door. It routes questions it cannot answer.

## When to ask a person

**Recommended.** Stop and refer the researcher to an office when any of
these holds:

- The answer is a decision only IU can make: signing an agreement, IRB or
  HIPAA review, an edge-case classification, export control, or an AI tool
  exception.
- Restricted or Critical data would go into an AI tool not approved for it.
- The answer depends on an open item in a skill.
- A deadline depends on the answer, so the office needs time to reply.
- Data may have been exposed. Report it as the note after the table says.

Then say plainly that the question needs a person. Name the office from the
table below, and what to send. Do not offer a best guess as the answer. Only
the office can decide, and a guess can lead a researcher to break an
agreement or a rule.

When no row fits, ask the RDC at iurdc@iu.edu, from the RDC contact page
in the table below, not the IU KB.

## Who decides what

**Recommended.** Contacts change, so give one only with its source. Where
the Source is an IU Knowledge Base (KB) article, read that article for the
current contact, and cite it. Where no KB article lists the contact, the
Source is the office's own page. Read that page, cite it, and say the
contact is not from the KB.

**Required.** "Immediately report" information security incidents, per IU
incident reporting under IT-12. [KB0025260](https://servicenow.iu.edu/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0025260) points to the reporting
page. A possible PHI exposure goes to it-incident@iu.edu, with a call to
812-855-8476 during business hours. That address and number are from the
[reporting page](https://informationsecurity.iu.edu/report-incident/index.html),
not the KB; [KB0022752](https://servicenow.iu.edu/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0022752) lists it-incident@iu.edu as the University
Information Security Office (UISO) address. Other sensitive data exposures
go through the UISO/UIPO intake form on the same page.

## What to send

**Recommended.** Put these in the first message. They save a round of
questions:

- The project, the PI, and the funding source or sponsor.
- The data: what it is, where it came from, and roughly how much.
- The classification, if known, and why. See `classifying-research-data`.
- Every agreement, license, or consent form that applies, attached or named.
- The IRB protocol number, if there is one.
- What you want to do, with whom, and by when.
- Who outside IU is involved, and in which country.

Do not send the data itself, or any sample of Restricted or Critical data, by
email. KB0023604 discourages emailing such data.

- **Open item.** The Data Management FAQ names export@iu.edu for export
