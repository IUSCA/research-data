# Getting help with research data

Verified 2026-10-04 against the IU pages named in Sources.

Send each question to the office that decides it. The RDC is the general
front door. It routes questions it cannot answer.

## Who decides what

| Question | Ask | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure whom to ask; data IU might have; catalog additions | Research Data Commons (RDC) | iurdc@iu.edu |
| Data management plans, repositories, finding secondary data (IU Bloomington and regional campuses) | IU Libraries Research Data Services | resdata@iu.edu |
| The same, for IU Indianapolis | University Library data services | dataserv@iu.edu |
| Signing a DUA, DSA, data license, or transfer agreement | Office for Research Administration (ORA), Research Contracting | Unfunded Agreement Request Form, through One.IU |
| Human subjects, consent, IRB approval | Human Research Protection Program (HRPP) | irb@iu.edu |
| Research data classification, AI tool exceptions | Data Steward for Research Data | datard@iu.edu |
| Sharing institutional data with a third party; third-party assessment | University Data Management Council | iudata@iu.edu |
| Secure storage and computing for sensitive data, CUI, contract security terms | SecureMyResearch | securemyresearch@iu.edu |
| PHI and HIPAA | HIPAA Privacy Officer | hipaa@iu.edu |
| Export control and foreign collaborators | Research Security Office | rsohelp@iu.edu |
| GDPR | IU GDPR contact | gdpr@iu.edu |
| FERPA (student data) | Data Steward for student data | DataStu@iu.edu |
| Patents, commercialization, CRADAs | Innovation and Commercialization Office | Through IU Research |
| A suspected data exposure | IU incident reporting | https://informationsecurity.iu.edu/report-incident/ |
| INPC, IU Health, or Eskenazi data | Regenstrief Data Services | askrds@regenstrief.org |
| ICPSR, RADaRS, HCUP | Social Science Research Commons (SSRC) | ssrc@iu.edu |
| A specific catalog dataset | The entry's Public Contact | See `finding-iu-research-data` |
| Clusters, storage systems, allocations | UITS Research Technologies | The `getting-help-from-research-technologies` skill in the research-technologies repository |

**Required.** "Immediately report" information security incidents, per IU
incident reporting under IT-12. A possible PHI exposure goes to
it-incident@iu.edu, with a call to 812-855-8476 during business hours. Other
sensitive data exposures go through the UISO/UIPO intake form on the same
page.

## What to send

**Recommended.** Put these in the first message. They save a round of
questions:

- The project, the PI, and the funding source or sponsor.
- The data: what it is, where it came from, and roughly how much.
- The classification, if known, and why. See `classifying-research-data`.
- Every agreement, license, or consent form that applies, attached or named.
- The IRB protocol number, if there is one.
- What you want to do, with whom, and by when.
- Who outside IU is involved, and in which country.

Do not send the data itself, or any sample of Restricted or Critical data, by
email. KB0023604 discourages emailing such data.
