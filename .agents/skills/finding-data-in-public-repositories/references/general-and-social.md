# Generalist, social science, government, and Earth science repositories

**Observed 2026-11-05** from each repository's own pages, linked in each
entry. Wording is summarized unless quoted. Access terms and size limits
change. Read the repository's page before telling anyone how to get access
or deposit.

Access levels follow the key in `SKILL.md`: **open**, **registered**,
**membership**, and **controlled**.

## Finding aids

These search across repositories. None hosts data.

- **re3data.org** is "a comprehensive registry of research data repositories
  that is global and covers all research disciplines." Filter by subject,
  country, certification, and access. Source: https://www.re3data.org/about
- **NIH's generalist repository list** names Dataverse, Dryad, Figshare,
  IEEE Dataport, Mendeley Data, Open Science Framework, Synapse, Vivli, and
  Zenodo. NIH calls the list "not exhaustive" and says it "does not
  recommend a specific generalist repository." NIH prefers domain-specific
  repositories where they exist. Source:
  https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/accessing-data/scientific
- **The Generalist Repository Ecosystem Initiative (GREI)** at NIH includes
  Dataverse, Dryad, Figshare, Mendeley Data, OSF, Vivli, and Zenodo. Source:
  https://datascience.nih.gov/data-ecosystem/generalist-repository-ecosystem-initiative
- **Google Dataset Search** indexes dataset metadata "hosted in thousands of
  repositories across the Web." Source:
  https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/help
- **Data.gov** is "The United States Government's open data site." It
  harvests federal agencies' listings into one catalog, managed by the U.S.
  General Services Administration. Each dataset's access follows the agency
  that publishes it. Sources: https://data.gov/about/,
  https://catalog.data.gov/

## Generalist repositories

All four take deposits from any field. Find data in them through the finding
aids above, or their own search.

| Repository | Default access | Deposit limits (Observed 2026-11-05) | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zenodo, run by CERN | Open; depositors may choose closed, embargoed, or restricted. Metadata is always public | 50 GB per record; higher quotas case by case | https://about.zenodo.org/policies/ |
| Dryad | Open, under a CC0 waiver. No personally identifiable data, and no human-subject transcripts, images, audio, or video | Files ideally under 10 GB; up to 2 TB per publication. A data publishing charge applies unless an institution sponsors it | https://datadryad.org/about, https://datadryad.org/help/requirements/costs |
| Figshare | Public with a DOI on publishing; embargo and restricted options | 20 GB private storage and 20 GB per file for a free account | https://info.figshare.com/user-guide/account-limits/ |
| Harvard Dataverse | Published datasets viewable without login; depositors may restrict files and take access requests | Not confirmed; varies by installation | https://dataverse.org/about, https://guides.dataverse.org/en/latest/user/find-use-data.html |
| OSF (Open Science Framework) | "Everything on OSF is private by default"; owners choose what is public | 5 GB per private project, 50 GB per public project | https://help.osf.io/article/137-osf-storage-caps |

OSF is also used to preregister a hypothesis and analysis plan, per
https://www.cos.io/products/osf.

## Social science and survey data

### ICPSR

- **Open item.** ICPSR's site returned HTTP 403 to scripts on 2026-11-05,
  so its own terms were not read. Its access levels, restricted-data
  applications, and the Virtual Data Enclave are unconfirmed here. Read
  https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/ in a browser. At IU, `using-licensed-and-public-data`
  covers ICPSR membership from IU pages.

### IPUMS (University of Minnesota)

- **Holds.** IPUMS USA "collects, preserves and harmonizes U.S. census
  microdata." IPUMS CPS holds Current Population Survey microdata. IPUMS
  International holds "census microdata from around the world." NHGIS holds
  "summary tables and time series" with GIS mapping files.
- **Access.** Registered and free. Users "must agree to terms of use when
  registering," valid for one year.
- **Required.** Per the IPUMS terms: do not redistribute the data without
  permission, cite it, do not use it to identify individuals, and keep
  extracts secure. IPUMS International bars commercial and genealogical use.
- Sources: https://www.ipums.org/about/terms, https://usa.ipums.org/usa/,
  https://international.ipums.org/international/, https://www.nhgis.org/

### Pew Research Center

- **Holds.** Survey datasets, such as American Trends Panel waves, Global
  Attitudes, and the Religious Landscape Study, released after a delay.
- **Access.** Registered: a free account to download.
- **Required.** Pew's terms require attribution and a disclaimer, and bar
  attempts to identify respondents. American Trends Panel data has its own
  terms.
- Sources: https://www.pewresearch.org/datasets/,
  https://www.pewresearch.org/about/terms-and-conditions/

### Roper Center for Public Opinion Research (Cornell)

- **Holds.** Public opinion data, including "a vast pre- and post-election
  polling collection" and state and national exit polls "back to 1972."
- **Access.** Membership. Members' affiliates get "Unlimited Access to those
  datasets." Non-members may order data for a fee. Some collections are
  free. Off-campus access is usually through a member library's proxy.
- Indiana University is on the Roper member list (Observed 2026-11-05).
  The IU inventory in `finding-iu-research-data` has its terms at IU.
- Sources: https://ropercenter.cornell.edu/membership,
  https://ropercenter.cornell.edu/membership/list-members,
  https://ropercenter.cornell.edu/quick-answers/faq

## U.S. federal statistics

### U.S. Census Bureau

- **data.census.gov** is "the U.S. Census Bureau's primary data search
  platform" for demographic and economic data. The Census Data API serves
  the same data to scripts. Source:
  https://www.census.gov/data/what-is-data-census-gov.html
- **Federal Statistical Research Data Centers (FSRDCs)** "provide secure
  environments supporting qualified researchers using restricted-access
  data." Access is controlled. Source: https://www.census.gov/about/adrm/fsrdc.html
- **The Standard Application Process (SAP)** is the portal for requesting
  restricted microdata from federal statistical agencies. The Census page
  says to contact an FSRDC administrator early, submit through the SAP, and
  then obtain Special Sworn Status, with a background check. It says Census
  review takes up to 12 weeks. Special Sworn Status needs 36 of the past 60
  months lived in the U.S. and a U.S.-based affiliation. Fees apply; see the
  Census SAP page. Sources:
  https://www.census.gov/topics/research/guidance/restricted-use-microdata/standard-application-process.html,
  https://app.sap.nsf.gov/help
- **Open item.** researchdatagov.org returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-05.
  Whether it still fronts the SAP, or now points to the NSF-hosted portal,
  is unconfirmed.

NHANES and CDC WONDER are in [biomedical.md](biomedical.md).

## Earth and environmental science

### NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

- **Holds.** Oceanic, atmospheric, and geophysical data, in "one of the
  most significant archives on Earth."
- **Access.** Open, through its Data Access application and APIs. The
  Climate Data Online API needs a free access token.
- **Deposit.** NCEI accepts data from researchers worldwide. Data not funded
  by NOAA goes through a scientific appraisal. Contact ncei.archive@noaa.gov
  first (from the [NCEI archive page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/archive), not the IU KB).
- Sources: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access, https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/archive

### NASA Earthdata

- **Holds.** "One of the planet's largest repositories of Earth science
  data," through NASA's Distributed Active Archive Centers.
- **Access.** Registered and free. "You will need to use Earthdata Login
  anytime you want to download Earth science data." NASA mission data is
  licensed CC0, and users are "very strongly urged to cite the data."
- Sources: https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/earthdata-login,
  https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software-policies/data-use-guidance

### USGS EarthExplorer

- **Holds.** Aerial photographs and satellite imagery of the Earth's
  surface, from the USGS EROS archive.
- **Access.** Registered. "You must be logged in to download and order
  scenes." Some data needs an end-user license agreement.
- Sources: https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/,
  https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-products-overview
